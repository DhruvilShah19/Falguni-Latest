# 🚀 Falguni Website: "Best-in-Class" Roadmap

To make Falguni Gruh Udhyog feel like a top-tier, enterprise-grade e-commerce brand (like Zepto, Blinkit, or premium D2C brands), we need to focus on **Trust, Security, and Frictionless UX**.

Here is the strategic plan to elevate the website:

## 1. Authentication & Security (The "Safe" Feel)
*   **Phone Number OTP Login (Highly Recommended over traditional 2FA):** 
    *   *Why:* In Indian e-commerce, traditional 2FA (passwords + SMS code) causes massive drop-offs. The "best-in-class" standard is **Passwordless Phone OTP Login**. It feels incredibly secure and modern (just like Swiggy or Zomato).
    *   *Alternative (True 2FA):* If you prefer traditional 2FA, we can enable Firebase Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), which requires users to enter a password first, then verifies an SMS code.
*   **FSSAI License & Trust Badges:** Prominently display your FSSAI license number, "100% Secure Checkout", and "Quality Guarantee" badges in the footer and on the product pages.

## 2. Customer Trust & Transparency (The "Legit" Feel)
*   **Dedicated Legal & Policy Pages:** A legitimate brand must have easily accessible, well-formatted pages for:
    *   Return & Refund Policy
    *   Shipping Policy
    *   Terms of Service & Privacy Policy
*   **WhatsApp Live Chat Support:** A floating WhatsApp widget in the bottom right corner gives customers instant confidence that a real team is behind the website ready to help them.
*   **Order Tracking Portal:** A dedicated `/tracking` page where users can enter their Order ID to see a live timeline of their order (Pending -> Processing -> On the Way).

## 3. Performance & SEO (The "Premium" Feel)
*   **Rich Snippets (Structured Data):** We inject Google Schema markup into the product pages. When people search for "Falguni snacks" on Google, the search results will display the price, stock status, and a 5-star rating directly in the Google Search page.
*   **Instant Search with Autocomplete:** Upgrading the search bar so it instantly suggests products and categories as the user types, tolerating minor typos (e.g., typing "kakhra" instead of "khakhra" still works).

## 4. Revenue & Conversion Boosters
*   **Abandoned Cart Recovery:** If a user leaves items in their cart, we can trigger a notification or send them a quick email/SMS to bring them back.
*   **"Frequently Bought Together" / Upsells:** Just before checkout, show a small section: *"Most people buying Khakhra also bought this Chutney."*
